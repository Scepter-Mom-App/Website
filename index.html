II The name

Why Scepter.

Esther had no parents, no power of her own, and everything on the line. When she walked into the king's court uninvited, he held out his golden scepter. It meant she was welcome. She had his favor, and she had his ear.

You have that same welcome. Not someday, when life calms down. Today, in the middle of it.

Yes. Every prayer is rooted in Scripture, and the verse is always shown so you can read it for yourself. We use the World English Bible, a modern, easy-to-read translation.

Yes. What you share stays between you and God. We never sell your information.
