II The name

Why Scepter.

Esther was an orphan, raised by her cousin, with no power of her own. When her people faced destruction, she knew what it could cost her to plead for them. In her day, anyone who walked into the king's throne room uninvited was put to death, unless the king held out his golden scepter.

Esther fasted and prayed for three days, then walked in anyway. "If I perish, I perish."

The king saw her and held out the scepter. That one gesture said: you may live, you are welcome, and I am listening. She came on behalf of the people she loved, and through her, they were saved.

You come on behalf of the people you love, too. And because of Jesus, you never have to wonder whether the scepter will be held out. It already has been. Scepter is named for that open door, every single day.

Yes. What you share stays between you and God. We never sell your information.

Yes. No contracts. You can cancel in a couple of taps.

Do I have to be a strong Christian?

Not at all. Whether you pray every day or haven't in years, you're welcome here.
